Challenge: A good apology

Practise the ingredients of a genuine apology. First for yourself, then for your partner.

You can download your answers if you like. Note: your words stay on this page. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere unless you choose to download or print.

Part 1: The apology you wish you'd heard

Think about a time you experienced a mild hurt in a relationship, not your most painful memory, just something that still niggles a little. Notice how it made you feel, and why it bothered you.

Using the ingredients below, write the apology you would have liked to receive from your partner.

Ingredients of a successful apology

  • Describe the offence and express remorse.
  • Acknowledge the impact of the offence.
  • Ask your partner what they need.
  • Where appropriate, explain how you'll make sure it doesn't happen again.

Part 2: Now write it the other way

Think about a time you mildly hurt your partner. Think about how it made them feel, and why it bothered them.

Using the same ingredients, write an apology to your partner to practise putting it into words.
