Looking beneath a reaction

Think of one recent moment when your partner did or said something and you reacted strongly. We'll work through it step by step, to see what was happening underneath. At the end, you can copy your answers to keep.

Choose a moment that felt small to medium, rather than your hardest one. There are no right or wrong answers. Choose any options that fit and add your own words if you like.

Your answers are private. Nothing you write is sent or saved anywhere. It stays on your device. If you leave this page, your answers will be cleared, so copy them before you leave.
